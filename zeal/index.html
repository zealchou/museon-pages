緣起

Origin

三十年，做同一件事

Thirty years, one craft

19 歲那年，我開始創業。往後的二十八年裡，我做過主題社群空間、社群旅遊平台、共同工作空間，也在顧問桌前看過上百家企業從谷底翻身——鮭魚貿易商三個月營收成長三倍，老街攤商從四個通路做到三百個。每一次翻身，表面上是營收數字，實際上都是同一件事：把流程拆開重建，把組織重新設計。而流程與組織的底層，藏著一個更根本的提問——這家公司的決策，究竟是怎麼形成的？

At nineteen, I started my first company. Over the twenty-eight years since, I've built community spaces, a social travel platform, and a co-working brand — and sat across the table from more than a hundred businesses climbing back from the edge. A salmon importer tripled revenue in three months. A street-market vendor grew from four retail channels to three hundred. On the surface, every turnaround was a revenue number. Underneath, it was always the same craft: taking processes apart and rebuilding them, and redesigning the organization around them. And beneath process and organization lies a more fundamental question — how does this company actually form its decisions?

崩盤，與重新提問

Collapse, and the question that followed

中間也不是一路順遂。31 歲那年，我一手創辦的社群旅遊平台跟我的婚姻，幾乎在同一年崩盤。那次崩盤逼我從頭問：企業的流程與組織，究竟該怎麼設計，判斷才不會失準？這到底有沒有方法可循，還是純粹憑經驗與運氣賭一把？往後十一年，我把這個問題帶到六十多種產業、上百家公司的顧問現場去驗證，也帶到超過三千位學員的課堂與教練現場去打磨。

It wasn't all upward. At thirty-one, the social travel platform I'd built and my marriage collapsed within the same year. That collapse forced me back to first principles: how should a company's processes and organization be designed so that judgment doesn't fail? Is there an actual method — or is it just experience and luck? For the next eleven years, I tested that question across more than sixty industries and a hundred companies as a consultant, and refined it in front of more than three thousand students as a teacher and coach.

方法的成形

A method takes shape

我把這些現場經驗收斂成兩套原創方法。「商業模式十二力」是一套結構性的商業健檢，逐力檢視商業模式哪裡撐得住、哪裡正在漏水；「萬謬卡」則看另一個維度——測一個人的能量狀態，如何影響他的人際關係與事情的結果。流程與組織是結構，人是能量；再造要成功，兩邊都得看見。

I distilled that field experience into two original methods. The Twelve Forces of Business Model is a structural health check — force by force, it shows where a business model holds and where it leaks. Wanmiu Cards reads a different dimension: how a person's energy state shapes their relationships and the outcomes of what they do. Process and organization are the structure; people are the energy. For any redesign to succeed, you have to see both.

交給 AI，做下去

Handing it to AI

2022 年底，我開始把這套邏輯拿去跟 AI 對話、測試，才發現：它其實可以被系統化。2026 年，Museon 原型成形——它的本質，是把「流程再造」與「組織再造」這兩件我手工做了三十年的事，交給 AI 系統化執行：一個會持續陪伴、記得脈絡、既懂結構也懂人的決策夥伴，讓更多人不必再花三十年繞遠路。

In late 2022, I started testing that logic in conversation with AI — and saw that it could be systematized. In 2026, the Museon prototype took shape. At its core, Museon takes the two things I've done by hand for thirty years — process redesign and organization redesign — and hands them to AI to execute systematically: a decision partner that stays with you, remembers context, and understands both structure and people, so no one has to take the thirty-year detour.

共事風格

Working style

清晨五點多，多數人還在睡，他已經坐在螢幕前開工了。跟他共事過的人都學會一條不成文的換算公式：別人一週的產出額度，他大概兩天半就用完。這種強度不是表演給誰看的——是使命驅動到近乎執著的副作用。他真心相信自己在做的事重要，重要到不太懂得喊停；那條「該休息了」的線，經常是身體先替他劃下的，而不是他自己。這是他最突出的引擎，也是他最真實的耗損。

Most mornings he's at his desk before sunrise. People who work with him learn an unofficial conversion rate: a normal week's worth of output, he burns through in about two and a half days. It isn't a performance — it's the side effect of being mission-driven to the point of obsession. He believes so completely in what he's building that he never quite learned how to stop; the line that says "enough for today" is usually drawn by his body, not by him. It is his most powerful engine, and his most honest cost.

開會時把一個半生不熟的方案放到他面前，你會先被三個問題釘住：「測過嗎？多少百分比？為什麼不是另一個選項？」他對含糊沒有耐性，對虛胖的簡報、不落地的漂亮話更是零容忍——這讓跟他共事的人壓力不小，因為他的標準幾乎不妥協。但他很少直接把答案丟給你。這是他教練魂的一面：他寧可多花二十分鐘問問題，把你逼到自己想清楚，也不願意花兩分鐘替你做結論。熬過那段追問的人，通常會發現自己的思考被磨利了一個檔次。

Bring him a half-baked proposal and you'll be pinned by three questions before you finish your first slide: Have you tested it? What's the percentage? Why not the other option? He has no patience for vagueness, and none at all for polished decks that never touch the ground — which makes working with him genuinely demanding, because his standards barely bend. Yet he rarely hands you the answer. That's the coach in him: he would rather spend twenty minutes asking questions that force you to think it through than two minutes drawing the conclusion for you. People who survive the questioning tend to come out thinking a notch sharper.

他的直球有時不留情面——話說出口先求真、再求圓，順序從來不會反過來。初次共事的人常常不太好受，長期共事的人卻因此省下大量猜測成本：他的桌面上沒有暗牌。而只要是他自己驗證過有效的方法，他幾乎藏不住——寫出來、教出去、分享給還在繞遠路的人，對他來說像呼吸一樣自然。跟他共事，你會同時遇見這兩個人：一個把標準架得極高、也把自己燒得太兇的人，和一個真心希望你變強的人。

His directness can land without much padding — truth first, tact second, never the other way around. First-timers often find it bruising; long-timers find it liberating, because there are no hidden cards on his table. And anything he has personally verified to work, he simply cannot keep to himself — he writes it down, teaches it, gives it away to whoever is still taking the long way around. Work with him and you meet both people at once: the one who sets the bar punishingly high and burns himself too hot, and the one who genuinely wants you to get stronger.

決策原則

Decision principles

  • 先問為什麼，再問怎麼做Ask why before how
  • 減法優先於加法Subtraction before addition
  • 沒有驗證過的宣告，不算完成An unverified claim isn't done
  • 拒絕含糊——沒有數字，就不是答案No number, no answer
  • 遇到方案，先問「為什麼不是另一個選項」Always ask "why not the alternative"
  • 同樣的判斷連續失準兩次，代表要重新診斷，不是硬撐Two misses in a row means re-diagnose, not push through
