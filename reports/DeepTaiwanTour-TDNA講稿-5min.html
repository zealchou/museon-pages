Deep Taiwan Tour

TDNA 台東大會 · 5 分鐘演講稿
主題：From Local to Global — Why Central Taiwan?

Act 1 — 開場：一個畫面

⏱ 0:00 – 0:40（約 40 秒）
Close your eyes for a moment.

Imagine waking up in a small town surrounded by tea fields. The air smells like roasted oolong. An 80-year-old farmer hands you a cup and says — in Taiwanese — "This tea has been here longer than any of us."

You take a sip. And suddenly, Taiwan isn't just a pin on your travel map anymore. It's a taste. A story. A person.

That's what Deep Taiwan Tour is about.
【中文對照】
閉上眼睛想像一下。你在茶園環繞的小鎮醒來，空氣聞起來是焙烏龍的味道。一位 80 歲的茶農遞給你一杯茶，用台語說：「這茶比我們任何人都老。」你喝了一口——台灣突然不再只是地圖上的一個點，而是一種味道、一個故事、一個人。這就是 Deep Taiwan Tour 在做的事。

Act 2 — 為什麼我做這件事

⏱ 0:40 – 2:00（約 80 秒）
My name is Shu-Hui, and I run a licensed travel company in central Taiwan.

For years, I traveled across Taiwan with my family — members who came from overseas. We climbed mountains together, explored coastlines, discovered hidden restaurants in small towns. We found Taiwan's beauty together — through each other's eyes.

Then, one by one, the elders in our family passed away. And those trips — the laughter at a roadside noodle stand, the surprise on my uncle's face seeing fireflies for the first time, the quiet mornings in the tea fields — became the most precious memories I have.

That's when I understood: travel isn't about destinations. It's about the people you share it with and the moments that stay after everything else fades.

I started Deep Taiwan Tour because I wanted to give others what my family gave me — not a checklist of attractions, but moments that become memories you carry forever.

I realized something: millions of travelers come to Taiwan every year. Most of them visit Taipei, take a photo at Jiufen, eat at the night market, and leave. They see Taiwan — but they never feel it.

Central Taiwan — Taichung, Nantou, Miaoli, Changhua — is where Taiwan's cultural heartbeat lives. But it's invisible to international travelers. Not because it's not worth seeing. Because no one has told its story in a way the world can hear.

That's my job. I translate local soul into global experience.
【中文對照】
我是淑慧，在中台灣經營一家合法旅行社。

好幾年來，我帶著從海外回來的家族成員，在台灣上山下海。我們一起發現台灣的美、台灣的美食——透過彼此的眼睛看這片土地。

後來，家族的長輩一個一個離開了。而那些旅行——路邊麵攤的笑聲、叔叔第一次看到螢火蟲時驚喜的表情、茶園裡安靜的早晨——都成了我最珍貴的回憶。

那一刻我懂了：旅行不是關於目的地，而是關於你跟誰一起、以及那些在一切褪去之後仍然留下的片刻。

我創辦 Deep Taiwan Tour，是因為我想把我家族給我的那種感受，也給其他人——不是景點清單，而是會變成一輩子記憶的時刻。

我發現一件事：每年數百萬旅客來台灣，大多數去台北、九份拍照、吃夜市，然後離開。他們「看到」台灣，但從來沒有「感受」過。

中台灣——台中、南投、苗栗、彰化——是台灣文化心跳的所在。但國際旅客看不見，不是因為不值得，而是因為沒有人用世界聽得懂的方式說這個故事。

這就是我的工作。把在地的靈魂，轉譯成國際的體驗。
💡 舞台提示：講家族故事這段放慢語速，「became the most precious memories I have」停頓一拍。這段是全篇的情感錨點——你在講自己的故事，不需要表演，真實就好。

Act 3 — 中台灣的文化底蘊

⏱ 2:00 – 3:30（約 90 秒）
Let me take you on a quick tour.

In Songboling, Nantou, tea farmers still process leaves by hand in a method unchanged for generations. When you sit in their living room, drinking tea they roasted that morning, you're not a tourist — you're a guest.

In Miaoli, there's a chocolate farm — yes, chocolate in Taiwan — where cacao grows in tropical soil and gets turned into bars you won't find anywhere else in the world.

In a small workshop, an artisan dips cotton cloth into indigo vats, fold by fold, creating patterns passed down through Hakka tradition. It takes three days to make one scarf. Three days of patience in a world that celebrates speed.

And on Mingde Reservoir, you can paddle a SUP board across water so still it reflects the mountains like a mirror. No crowds. No noise. Just you and the landscape.

This is central Taiwan. It's not loud. It's not Instagram-famous. But it's real.
【中文對照】
帶你快速走一圈。

在南投松柏嶺，茶農依然用世代傳承的手法製茶。坐在他們客廳裡，喝著當天早上烘的茶，你不是觀光客——你是客人。

在苗栗，有一座巧克力農場——對，台灣也產巧克力——可可在熱帶土壤中生長，做出的巧克力你在世界上任何其他地方都找不到。

在一個小工作坊，匠人把棉布一折一折浸入藍染缸，圖案源自客家傳統。做一條圍巾要三天。在這個追求速度的世界裡，三天的耐心。

在明德水庫，你可以划著 SUP 穿過靜得像鏡子一樣的水面，倒映著山。沒有人潮，沒有噪音，只有你和風景。

這就是中台灣。不喧鬧，不是 IG 網紅打卡點，但它是真的。

Act 4 — 為什麼你該來

⏱ 3:30 – 4:30（約 60 秒）
Now, as digital nomads, you've probably been to Bali, Chiang Mai, Lisbon, Mexico City. Amazing places. But here's what makes Taiwan different:

Taiwan is one of the safest countries in Asia. It has world-class infrastructure — high-speed rail, universal healthcare, 7-Elevens on every corner. And yet, twenty minutes outside any major city, you're in a completely different world — mountains, temples, farmers, artisans — living at a pace that makes you breathe.

Central Taiwan is the best of both worlds. You get the convenience of a modern city and the depth of a culture that has been quietly building for centuries.

And here's the thing most people don't know: Taiwan's licensed tour guides — like my team — are trained to take you to places Google Maps can't. The back alleys, the family workshops, the conversations you'd never have on your own.
【中文對照】
身為數位遊牧者，你們可能去過峇里島、清邁、里斯本、墨西哥城。都是很棒的地方。但台灣不一樣：

台灣是亞洲最安全的國家之一。有世界級的基礎建設——高鐵、全民健保、到處都有便利商店。但離開任何大城市 20 分鐘，你就進入一個完全不同的世界——山、廟、農人、匠人——活在一個讓你能「呼吸」的節奏裡。

中台灣兩全其美。你享受現代城市的便利，同時擁有一個靜靜累積了幾百年的文化深度。

還有一件大多數人不知道的事：台灣的合法導遊——像我的團隊——受過專業訓練，帶你去 Google Maps 找不到的地方。後巷、家庭工坊、你一個人不會有的對話。

Act 5 — 結尾：邀請

⏱ 4:30 – 5:00（約 30 秒）
So here's my invitation.

Tomorrow, come find me at the café. I brought a piece of central Taiwan with me — tea from Songboling, chocolate from Miaoli, handmade indigo cloth, and stories.

If you've ever wondered what Taiwan feels like — not the guidebook version, but the real, lived-in, deeply human version — come have a cup of tea with me. I'll show you.

My name is Shu-Hui, and I'm from Deep Taiwan Tour. Thank you.
【中文對照】
所以這是我的邀請。

明天，來咖啡廳找我。我帶了一小片中台灣來——松柏嶺的茶、苗栗的巧克力、手工藍染布，還有故事。

如果你曾經好奇台灣「感覺」起來是什麼樣子——不是旅遊書的版本，而是真實的、有人住的、深刻的人的版本——來喝杯茶。我說給你聽。

我是淑慧，Deep Taiwan Tour。謝謝大家。
💡 舞台提示：結尾時放慢語速，「come have a cup of tea with me」這句是全篇的情感高點，停頓一拍再說 I'll show you。如果手邊有茶，這時候可以舉起杯子。
